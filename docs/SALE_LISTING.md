# Draft Acquisition Listing — MarginGuard

## Product name
MarginGuard — Pricing & Profitability Studio

## Category and stage
Business Productivity / Developer Tools; functional browser MVP, pre-launch.

## Short description
A local-first pricing and profitability workbench that calculates contribution margin, estimates break-even and target prices, surfaces offers under their margin target, and preserves named pricing scenarios.

## Problem solved
Small SaaS operators, agencies, and digital-product sellers often price based on a simple markup and miss processing fees, delivery costs, or allocated overhead. MarginGuard consolidates pricing assumptions and makes the modeled per-sale outcome easier to compare across a product portfolio.

## Included functionality
- Dashboard with estimated monthly revenue/profit, weighted margin, price-position chart, and portfolio-health view.
- Product CRUD with direct cost, fulfillment, fixed transaction fee, percentage platform fee, overhead, target margin, and estimated monthly volume.
- Live Price Lab for price, discount, additional-cost, break-even, and target-price scenarios.
- Saved scenarios, search/filter, CSV export, JSON backup/restore, preferences, and demo reset.
- Source and technical handoff documentation, browser smoke tests, and CI workflow.

## Technology
HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, localStorage, Playwright browser tests, GitHub Actions, static hosting.

## Commercial status — disclose clearly
No customers, users, recurring revenue, verified sales volume, production backend, or paid integrations are included. The workspace is stored in the visitor's browser; there is no account system, cloud synchronization, billing, scheduled processing, or remote backup. Currency selection changes formatting only. Sample records are fictional.

## Suggested buyer
A small software studio, agency-tool vendor, SaaS operator, or productivity/fintech product company looking for a starter codebase to connect to hosted accounts, real sales data, billing, and team workflows.

## Transfer checklist
- Transfer repository administration and source files.
- Recreate hosting and verify the buyer-owned deployment.
- Sign a written IP assignment or commercial license.
- Review the included evaluation license and third-party components.
- Do not represent the sample data as real traction.

## Valuation note
As a pre-revenue prototype, a price should be anchored to code/documentation reuse value, demonstrable functionality, and actual buyer demand. No independent valuation or market-demand validation has been performed.
