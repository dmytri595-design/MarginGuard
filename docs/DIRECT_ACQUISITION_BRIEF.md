# MarginGuard — Direct Acquisition Brief

## Acquisition proposal

**MarginGuard — Pricing & Profitability Studio** is a functional, browser-local pricing and contribution-margin workbench offered for a one-time cash acquisition or a written commercial license.

- Demo: https://marginguard-studio.vercel.app
- Source repository: https://github.com/dmytri595-design/MarginGuard
- Current stage: pre-launch / pre-revenue. No customers, sales, MRR, or production backend are claimed.
- Price: open to a serious direct offer; no independent valuation or completed sale is represented.

## What the buyer can inspect

- Product portfolio with per-product pricing assumptions, cost and fee breakdowns, target margin, and estimated monthly volume.
- Price Lab with discounts, additional costs, break-even estimates and target-price calculations.
- Named what-if scenarios and baseline comparison.
- Search/status filters, CSV export, JSON workspace backup/restore, preferences, demo reset and print-friendly UI.
- Self-contained static application and technical/QA handoff documentation.
- Browser-based state: current saved data is in the visitor's localStorage only.

## Explicit product boundaries

MarginGuard does not connect to payment providers, bank feeds, accounting software, tax services or real transaction data. It has no login, cloud sync, database, billing, team sharing or remote backups. Currency settings only change display formatting; they do not convert amounts. Demo values are synthetic. Calculations exclude taxes, refunds, acquisition costs and any expenses the user has not entered. It is decision support, not accounting or tax advice.

## Rights and license

The repository contains a **restrictive evaluation notice**, not an open-source license. Repository visibility alone does not grant commercial use or derivative-work rights. Any buyer would need a written agreement that clearly identifies the assets and rights being assigned or licensed, the effective date, consideration, exclusions and any third-party components.

The seller should verify authorship and transfer rights for all included assets before closing. The sale must not imply that a Vercel URL, GitHub account or domain is automatically transferred; those are separate handover items to confirm with the buyer.

## Suggested handover

1. Buyer reviews the live demo and public repository.
2. Both sides agree on price and the precise scope of the asset/IP transfer in writing.
3. Payment and transfer conditions are secured before changing repository or hosting ownership.
4. The buyer deploys the source under an account they control and verifies it before closing the transition.

This is an acquisition asset, not a profitable operating business. An initial enquiry does not commit either party to a transaction.
