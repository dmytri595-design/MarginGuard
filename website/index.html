CURRENT SCOPE

Built to be useful. Clear about its limits.

Browser-local profitability workbench. Figures are user-entered or illustrative; taxes, refunds, chargebacks, acquisition costs and unentered costs are not modelled. Not accounting or tax advice.
GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered plainly.

Does MarginGuard replace accounting software?

No. It is a pricing decision-support prototype, not accounting, tax or bookkeeping software.

Where is data saved?

Product assumptions and saved scenarios are stored in the current browser profile. Export a JSON backup before clearing browser storage.

Are taxes and refunds included?

No. Taxes, refunds, chargebacks, acquisition cost and costs not entered by the user are excluded.
